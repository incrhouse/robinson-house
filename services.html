Crisis Support

Stabilization when it matters most

Crisis support at Robinson House provides short-term, intensive stabilization for individuals and families experiencing acute mental health crises. Dr. Cleve helps clients move from overwhelm to stability with a clear, structured plan for what comes next.

If you or someone you know is in immediate danger, call 988 or 911. Crisis support services are for non-emergency clinical stabilization.

  • Short-term intensive support for acute mental health crises
  • Safety planning and stabilization
  • Coordination with outpatient and higher levels of care
  • Available to existing clients in all licensed states
